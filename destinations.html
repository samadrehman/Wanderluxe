When to go

Best time to visit, at a glance

India has three travel seasons. Each destination page carries its own month-by-month guide — here is the short version.

WinterOctober – March
  • Rajasthan & the Golden Triangle 18–28°C
  • Kerala & Tamil Nadu 24–32°C
  • Goa & the Andamans 22–31°C
  • Dubai & the Maldives 24–30°C
SummerApril – June
  • Ladakh & Spiti 10–22°C
  • Kashmir & Himachal 15–28°C
  • Meghalaya & Sikkim 16–24°C
  • Switzerland & Europe 16–26°C
MonsoonJuly – September
  • Western Ghats & Coorg lush & green
  • Valley of Flowers blooming
  • Udaipur & Bundi dramatic skies
  • Bali & Thailand shoulder season
Good to know: monsoon is not a write-off. It is the best-value season for luxury hotels in the Western Ghats, and the most dramatic light for photographers.
